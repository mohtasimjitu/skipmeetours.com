About Us

Skip Mee Tours & Travels

Skip Mee Tours & Travels is a Malaysia-based travel and student consultancy agency built to make the journey between Malaysia, Bangladesh, and the world simple, honest, and stress-free.

From air tickets and visa processing to hotel bookings, tour packages, airport transfers, and full student consultancy, our team handles the details so you can focus on the destination — not the paperwork.

We also run a dedicated Malaysia ↔ Bangladesh courier service, keeping documents and parcels moving securely between the two countries our clients call home.
